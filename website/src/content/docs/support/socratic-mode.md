## Overview

Socratic Mode is ReadAny's unique AI-powered learning feature that uses the Socratic method to guide you through books with thought-provoking questions instead of direct answers. This approach helps you develop critical thinking and deeper understanding.

## What is Socratic Mode?

Unlike traditional AI chat that provides direct answers, Socratic Mode:
- **Asks guiding questions** to help you discover insights yourself
- **Encourages critical thinking** rather than passive reading
- **Adapts to your responses** to provide personalized guidance
- **Builds understanding step by step** through dialogue

## Enabling Socratic Mode

1. Open **Settings → AI**
2. Under **Chat Mode**, select **Socratic**
3. Configure Socratic settings:
   - **Enable Preheating**: Allow AI to prepare context before you start reading
   - **Preheating Strategy**: Choose how the AI prepares (Auto/Smart/Manual)
4. Click **Save**

## Using Socratic Mode

### Starting a Reading Session

1. Open any book in your library
2. The Socratic interface will appear automatically (if enabled)
3. You'll see:
   - Current chapter content
   - AI-generated guiding questions
   - Your response area

### Interacting with the AI

**The AI will ask you questions like:**
- "What do you think the author means by this passage?"
- "How does this concept relate to what we discussed earlier?"
- "Can you identify the main argument in this paragraph?"
- "What evidence supports this claim?"

**Your responses:**
- Type your thoughts in the response area
- The AI will analyze your answer
- Follow-up questions will adapt based on your understanding
- No right or wrong answers - it's about exploration

### Question Types

The Socratic AI generates different types of questions:

| Type | Purpose | Example |
|------|---------|---------|
| **Comprehension** | Check understanding | "What is the main idea here?" |
| **Analysis** | Deepen insight | "Why did the character make this choice?" |
| **Application** | Connect concepts | "How could you apply this in real life?" |
| **Evaluation** | Critical judgment | "Do you agree with the author's conclusion?" |
| **Synthesis** | Combine ideas | "How does this relate to chapter 3?" |

## Preheating Strategies

Preheating allows the AI to prepare context before you engage with the text, making the dialogue more relevant and insightful.

### Auto Strategy
- **When**: Automatically activates for every new chapter
- **Best for**: Beginners or complex texts
- **Behavior**: Always prepares context without asking

### Smart Strategy
- **When**: Activates only if you haven't seen the preheating dialog before
- **Best for**: Regular readers who want efficiency
- **Behavior**: Shows preheating dialog once per book, then works silently

### Manual Strategy
- **When**: Only when you explicitly trigger it
- **Best for**: Advanced users who want full control
- **Behavior**: Never auto-activates; you decide when to preheat

4. **Encourage reflection** - Use follow-up questions to deepen thinking

### For Book Clubs

## Troubleshooting

### AI Not Asking Questions

**Problem**: The AI provides direct answers instead of questions.

**Solutions**:
1. Verify Socratic mode is enabled in Settings → AI
2. Check that your API key is valid
3. Try restarting the reader
4. Ensure you're using a capable model (GPT-4, Claude, etc.)

### Questions Too Easy/Hard

**Problem**: Questions don't match your level.

**Solutions**:
1. Provide feedback: "This is too easy" or "This is too difficult"
2. The AI will adapt over time
3. Try a different book to reset context
4. Manually adjust difficulty in settings (if available)

### Preheating Not Working

**Problem**: Preheating doesn't activate as expected.

**Solutions**:
1. Check preheating strategy setting
2. For Smart strategy: clear the "has shown dialog" flag in settings
3. For Manual strategy: look for the preheat button in the toolbar
4. Verify AI provider is configured correctly

## Best Practices

### Getting Started
1. Start with a familiar book to understand the flow
2. Respond naturally - don't overthink
3. Experiment with different question types
4. Review your conversation history regularly

### Maximizing Learning
1. Use consistently - regular practice improves results
2. Take notes on insights you discover
3. Revisit challenging passages with AI guidance
4. Combine with traditional note-taking

### Avoiding Common Pitfalls
1. Don't treat it like a quiz - there are no grades
2. Don't skip questions - each builds on the previous
3. Don't expect immediate mastery - learning takes time
4. Don't use for simple factual queries - use regular AI chat instead

## Comparison: Socratic vs Regular Chat

| Feature | Socratic Mode | Regular Chat |
|---------|--------------|--------------|
| **Approach** | Asks questions | Provides answers |
| **Goal** | Develop thinking | Get information |
| **Best for** | Learning & understanding | Quick facts & summaries |
| **Interaction** | Multi-turn dialogue | Q&A format |
| **Depth** | Deep exploration | Surface-level answers |
| **Time** | Takes longer | Faster responses |

## Feedback and Improvement

The Socratic AI learns from your interactions:
- Rate responses (thumbs up/down)
- Provide text feedback
- Report inappropriate questions
- Suggest better questions

Your feedback helps improve future sessions.

## Privacy

All Socratic conversations:
- Are stored locally on your device
- Are not sent to ReadAny servers
- Only use your configured AI provider
- Can be deleted at any time
- Are included in your data export options
