## Overview

Mini Reviews are concise, AI-generated snapshots that capture the essence of what you're reading. They provide quick insights, thought-provoking hooks, and memorable takeaways to enhance your reading experience.

## What Are Mini Reviews?

Mini Reviews are short, focused comments generated by AI that highlight different aspects of a book:
- **Hook** - Engaging opening lines that draw you in
- **Question** - Thought-provoking questions to deepen reflection
- **Resonance** - Emotional connections and personal insights
- **Anecdote** - Interesting stories or examples from the text

Each mini review is designed to be brief yet impactful, helping you remember and reflect on key moments.

## Types of Mini Reviews

### 1. Hook (钩子)
**Purpose**: Capture attention with compelling openings

**Characteristics**:
- Intriguing statements
- Surprising facts
- Powerful quotes
- Dramatic revelations

**Example**: "In this chapter, the author reveals a shocking truth about human nature that challenges everything we thought we knew."

### 2. Question (问题)
**Purpose**: Stimulate critical thinking and reflection

**Characteristics**:
- Open-ended inquiries
- Philosophical prompts
- Personal application questions
- Comparative analyses

**Example**: "How would your life change if you applied the principle discussed in this passage?"

### 3. Resonance (共鸣)
**Purpose**: Create emotional connections with the text

**Characteristics**:
- Personal reflections
- Universal themes
- Emotional responses
- Relatable experiences

**Example**: "This moment captures the universal struggle between duty and desire that we all face."

### 4. Anecdote (轶事)
**Purpose**: Highlight interesting stories and examples

**Characteristics**:
- Memorable incidents
- Illustrative examples
- Historical parallels
- Character insights

**Example**: "The author shares a fascinating story about a scientist who made a breakthrough through accidental discovery."

## Using Mini Reviews

### Viewing Mini Reviews

Mini reviews appear in several places:
1. **Stats Dashboard** - View recent mini reviews for your current books
2. **Book Details** - Access all mini reviews for a specific book
3. **Smart Review Panel** - Browse curated reviews across your library

### Generating Mini Reviews

Mini reviews are automatically generated when:
- You finish a chapter
- You reach significant milestones
- You manually trigger generation
- The AI detects noteworthy passages

**Manual Generation**:
1. Open a book
2. Navigate to the Stats or Smart Review panel
3. Click "Generate Mini Review"
4. Select the type (hook/question/resonance/anecdote)
5. Wait for AI generation (usually 5-10 seconds)

### Customizing Default Type

Set your preferred mini review type:
1. Go to **Settings → Reading**
2. Find **Mini Review Settings**
3. Choose default type: Hook, Question, Resonance, or Anecdote
4. Save settings

The default type will be used when generating reviews without explicit selection.

## Managing Mini Reviews

### Pinning Reviews

Pin important mini reviews to keep them at the top:
1. Find the mini review you want to pin
2. Click the pin icon 📌
3. Pinned reviews appear first in lists
4. Click again to unpin

### Rating Reviews

Provide feedback on mini review quality:
- 👍 Thumbs up for helpful reviews
- 👎 Thumbs down for irrelevant reviews
- Your ratings help improve future generations

### Deleting Reviews

Remove mini reviews you don't find useful:
1. Hover over the review
2. Click the delete/trash icon
3. Confirm deletion
4. Deleted reviews cannot be recovered

## Troubleshooting

### Reviews Not Generating

**Problem**: Clicking generate does nothing or shows error.

**Solutions**:
1. Verify AI provider is configured (Settings → AI)
2. Check internet connection
3. Ensure book has been vectorized (for semantic context)
4. Try a different AI model
5. Check API quota/credits

### Poor Quality Reviews

**Problem**: Reviews are generic, irrelevant, or repetitive.

**Solutions**:
1. Rate poorly (thumbs down) to train the AI
2. Delete and regenerate
3. Try a different review type
4. Move to a different chapter
5. Provide more specific feedback

### Missing Review Types

**Problem**: Can't select certain review types.

**Solutions**:
1. Check settings for enabled types
2. Update ReadAny to latest version
3. Verify AI model supports all types
4. Some types may not suit all genres

### Sync Issues

**Problem**: Mini reviews not syncing across devices.

**Solutions**:
1. Check sync status (Settings → Sync)
2. Force sync manually
3. Verify WebDAV/S3 configuration
4. Check for sync conflicts
5. Review sync logs for errors
