ReadAny used Wiktextract and its deterministic pack builder to retain headwords,
parts of speech, Chinese pronunciation when present, Simplified/Traditional
forms, and non-empty definitions. It removed examples, translations, audio,
images, and other fields not represented by the ReadAny dictionary schema.
